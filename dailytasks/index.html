# Privacy Policy for DailyTasks **Effective date:** October 1, 2026 **App name:** DailyTasks **Developer:** NV-prasad ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how DailyTasks ("the App") handles information when you use the application. DailyTasks is a simple task-management application designed to help users organize everyday activities. Your privacy is important to us, and we aim to collect as little information as necessary to provide the App's functionality. ## 2. Information We Collect DailyTasks does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to use its core features. The App allows you to create, edit, complete, and delete tasks. Task information you enter is stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photographs, or other personal information through the App's core functionality. ## 3. How Your Information Is Used Task information stored on your device is used solely to provide the App's features, including displaying and managing your tasks and tracking your task-completion progress. The App does not transmit your task information to a server as part of its core functionality. ## 4. Data Storage and Security Your tasks are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud account or server for synchronizing your tasks. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored tasks. Although we take a privacy-conscious approach, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 5. Sharing of Information We do not sell, rent, or share your task information with third parties as part of the App's core functionality. If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices. ## 6. Permissions The App requests only the Android permissions needed for its functionality. If future versions require additional permissions, their purpose will be explained in the App or updated documentation as appropriate. ## 7. Children's Privacy DailyTasks is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App's core functionality. If you believe that personal information has been collected in connection with the App, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed. ## 8. Data Retention and Deletion Task information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Because the App's core functionality does not send task information to our servers, we generally do not hold a separate server-side copy of your tasks to delete. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available at the public privacy-policy URL associated with the App. ## 10.
