Privacy Policy for DailyTasks

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Developer: NV-prasad

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DailyTasks ("the App") handles information when you use the application. DailyTasks helps users organize their everyday activities. We aim to collect as little information as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

2. Information We Collect

DailyTasks does not require an account, sign-in, or personal information to use its core features. Tasks you create, edit, complete, or delete are stored locally on your device.

The App's core functionality does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photographs.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored task information is used to display and manage your tasks and track task-completion progress. The App's core functionality does not send task information to a server.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your tasks are stored locally on your device. The App does not provide cloud synchronization or a cloud account. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it may permanently remove locally stored tasks. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your task information. The App's core functionality does not share your tasks with third parties. If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated as necessary.

6. Permissions

The App uses the Android permissions required for its functionality. Any additional permissions introduced in future versions will be assessed and explained as appropriate.

7. Children's Privacy

DailyTasks is a general-purpose productivity application. The App's core functionality does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you have concerns about information handling, please contact the developer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Tasks remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. The App's core functionality does not maintain a separate server-side copy of your tasks.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the public privacy-policy URL associated with DailyTasks.
